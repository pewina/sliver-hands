# SilverHands — Skill → Profile → Recommendation → Collaboration Demo

This build keeps the live ElevenLabs voice pipeline intact and makes the rest of the hackathon journey depend on the detected skills.

## End-to-end demo

1. **Voice onboarding**
   - Speak naturally in Tamil/English.
   - ElevenLabs Scribe v2 transcribes the recording.
   - The backend extracts skills, experience and language.

2. **Profile creation**
   - When the user taps `Explore opportunities`, the detected skills become the SilverHands profile.
   - The profile screen shows the detected skills, experience, transcript and an AI profile summary.
   - If the backend/Gemini profile endpoint is available, the headline and bio are generated by Gemini; otherwise a local safe fallback is used.

3. **Personalised recommendations**
   - Demo mode scores every opportunity against the detected skills.
   - Skill compatibility is the strongest signal, followed by local demand, seasonal demand, verification and experience.
   - Example: `Cooking` pushes tiffin services and festival food orders to the top.
   - Example: `Tailoring` pushes blouse stitching and alteration work to the top.
   - Example: `Embroidery/Handicrafts` pushes handmade and embroidery orders to the top.

4. **Interested → Match**
   - Tapping `Interested` stores the choice in the demo state.
   - A business match screen opens with the selected opportunity's actual business, price, requirements and match score.

5. **Collaboration**
   - Bulk opportunities expose an `AI collaboration` plan.
   - The user's detected skills are shown as the user's contribution.
   - Compatible partner sellers are selected from the relevant skill pool.
   - Contributions are allocated until the order is fulfilled.
   - `Create Business Squad` opens the live contribution dashboard.

- Cooking / related food skills
- Tamil
- 10 years (when the backend extracts the Tamil number correctly)
- Food opportunities ranked first
- Bulk food order can open AI collaboration
